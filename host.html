About Us

LiraUniHostels

Connecting Lira University students with quality, affordable accommodation through intelligent AI-powered recommendations.

Our Mission

LiraUniHostels aims to solve the accommodation challenges faced by Lira University students by providing a centralized, AI-powered platform where they can discover, compare, and book quality housing with confidence — saving time and reducing stress during their academic journey.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted student accommodation platform in Northern Uganda — where every Lira University student can find a safe, comfortable, and affordable place to call home throughout their studies.
